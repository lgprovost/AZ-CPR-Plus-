Onsite Training Questions

Where can you provide onsite CPR training?

We travel to workplaces, schools and private group locations in Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler and throughout Arizona. All current training is delivered in person at your location; we do not operate a public classroom facility.

Do you bring the training equipment?

Yes. CPR Plus+ brings the training equipment and instruction to your location. Share details about your space and the number of participants so we can discuss the setup for your course.

How many people can attend a group course?

Our minimum group size is 10 participants. Include your estimated attendance in the quote form so we can confirm instructor arrangements and class-size limits for your program. We also offer individual training by arrangement.

Can we request evening or weekend training?

Evening and weekend arrangements are available to discuss. Send your preferred dates and times with your request so we can check instructor availability.

What information do you need for a group quote?

Tell us the course you need, your organization's name, participant count, location and preferred dates. Include any required certification issuer or workplace training requirements. You can compare our training courses before submitting your request.
