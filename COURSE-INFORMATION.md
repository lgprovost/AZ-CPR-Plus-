# CPR Plus+ course information reference

Reviewed September 14, 2026. This is an internal reference for maintaining website
copy, not a promise of the duration or availability of a particular booking.
The linked HSI program pages and specification sheets are the source documents;
competitor descriptions and online-only course durations were not used as proxies
for CPR Plus+'s onsite classes.

## Confirmed business details

- Legal business name: CPR Plus Emergency Medical Training.
- Certification providers: HSI, American Heart Association (AHA) and American Red
  Cross, depending on the course. Confirm the issuer for each booking.
- The supplied `ahi_training.png` logo represents American Safety & Health
  Institute (ASHI). It is displayed alongside HSI, AHA and American Red Cross on
  the home page. ASHI belongs to HSI's training brand family; the added logo does
  not establish a new course offering. See the official
  [HSI brand-transition fact sheet](https://goto.hsi.com/hubfs/HSI%202020%20Ecare%20Program%20Materials/HSI%202020%20Fact%20sheet.pdf).
- Delivery: instructors travel to customer-selected sites and workplaces for
  in-person training.
- Minimum group size: 10 participants; individual training is also available.
- No public classroom facility currently; individual sessions are arranged onsite.
- Two-person CPR practice is an emphasis of CPR Plus+ instruction.
- The initial 2–4 hour estimate needs confirmation by course; it does not cover all
  programs in the current website's course list.

Successful completion requirements vary. The First Aid / CPR AED specification
sheet requires competent skills performance; written exams depend on the course
and applicable requirements. BLS includes skills and written evaluation. An HSI
credential should not be advertised as an AHA-issued card or universally accepted
by every employer.

## Items to confirm before publishing more specific promises

- Exact course variants, initial/renewal times, preparation and enrollment rules
  offered by CPR Plus+. HSI's broader delivery options do not establish that CPR
  Plus+ offers online, blended or remote-video instruction.
- Current PALS availability, curriculum, issuer details and prerequisites: an
  [official ASHI PALS specification sheet](https://goto.hsi.com/hubfs/ASHI-Medic%202017/HSI8119_ASHI%20PALS_spec%20sheet_web.pdf)
  exists, but it is dated 2017; obtain current program documentation before using
  its durations or requirements as current website claims.
- Certificate type, duration and prerequisites for High-Performance CPR, Medical
  Scenarios, Stop the Bleed, HP Bleeding Control, Narcan and Crisis De-Escalation.
  Do not assume every specialty offering issues the same two-year HSI card.
- Pediatric first aid needs for childcare clients should be matched to the
  appropriate program rather than assumed covered by adult first aid.
- Two-person CPR can be highlighted as a training emphasis. Avoid exclusivity
  claims: HSI's BLS materials explicitly include multiple-provider CPR.
