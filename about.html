Our Story

Founder Brad Leakey began teaching CPR and first aid for the Phoenix Fire Department in 1987. In 1991, the firefighter and paramedic founded CPR Plus+ to bring practical emergency response training to individuals, workplaces, healthcare providers and schools.

Our Instructors

Many of our instructors are active or former firefighters and paramedics. Their field experience informs hands-on practice, realistic scenarios and clear explanations. We offer certification options through HSI/ASHI, the American Heart Association and the American Red Cross, depending on the course, and emphasize two-person CPR practice.

Where We Train

We provide training throughout Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler and the rest of Arizona. We work with businesses, healthcare teams, schools, community groups and sports organizations. Our service is onsite-only, with individual training available and a minimum of 10 participants for group courses.
