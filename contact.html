Our Mission

Expert Training from the
Front Lines

AZ CPR Plus+ has been a trusted leader in CPR, AED, and First Aid training for decades. Founder Brad Leakey began instructing CPR and First Aid for the Phoenix Fire Department in 1987, bringing real-world emergency response experience into the classroom. In 1991, Firefighter/Paramedic Brad Leakey founded AZ CPR Plus+ with the mission of providing high-quality, practical life-saving training to individuals, businesses, healthcare providers, schools, and organizations. Today, AZ CPR Plus+ delivers training not only throughout the Phoenix metropolitan area, but also across Arizona and nationwide. We are proud to be affiliated with numerous professional sports organizations within the NHL, NBA, WNBA, and MLB. Our instructors are certified through leading organizations such as the American Heart Association (AHA) and the American Safety & Health Institute (ASHI), and many are active or former firefighters and paramedics. This combination of professional certifications and real-life emergency response experience allows our instructors to go beyond simply teaching a course, they provide students with the knowledge, confidence, and practical understanding needed to respond effectively during real emergencies. That commitment to excellence and hands-on expertise is what sets AZ CPR Plus+ apart from the average CPR training company.

Brad Leakey
